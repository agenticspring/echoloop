ECHOLOOP

a 12-second time-loop survival arena

Grab the green orbs to score — each orb raises your combo.
Every 12 seconds a red ghost awakens and replays your exact moves from the last loop.
Touch a ghost and you die. Ghosts fade after 3 loops — but new ones keep coming.
Survive. Outsmart yourself.
